01 — ABOUT US

Insurance should be simple, explicit and human.

Morning Bird Insurance Services is a legally registered insurance intermediary. We provide service, consultancy and advisory across insurance, connecting clients with carefully selected insurance providers.

Our niche is general insurance, with a strong and special bias towards Motor and Health Insurance. We aim to make insurance a common-place service that is understandable and accessible to every Kenyan.
