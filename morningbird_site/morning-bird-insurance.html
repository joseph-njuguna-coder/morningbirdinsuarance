What we cover

Six kinds of cover, one call to arrange.

We place your risk with the underwriter best suited to it, then manage the policy and any claim on your behalf.

Motor Insurance

Third-party or comprehensive cover for private, commercial and PSV vehicles, with a repair-network of your choosing.

Medical Cover

Inpatient, outpatient and maternity plans for individuals, families and groups, with access to hospitals nationwide.

Travel Insurance

Medical emergencies, lost luggage, flight delay and cancellation cover for business trips, holidays and study abroad.

Home Insurance

Cover for the building, contents or both, against fire, theft, flooding and other damage — in Nairobi or upcountry.

Life & Investment

Life assurance and endowment plans that protect your family's income today and build savings for later.

Personal Accident

A lump-sum payout for death or permanent disability following an accident, on top of any medical cover you hold.
