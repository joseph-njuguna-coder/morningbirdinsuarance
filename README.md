# Morning Bird Insurance Services 🦅🇰🇪

An independent insurance agency web application built for **Morning Bird Insurance Services**, an intermediary regulated by the Insurance Regulatory Authority (IRA) in Kenya. 

- **Live Site:** [https://morningbirdinsurance.co.ke](https://morningbirdinsurance.co.ke)

The platform is designed to provide clear information on cover options (Motor, Medical, Marine, Fire, and Corporate), display helpful insurance insights via a built-in journal/blog, and capture prospective leads through an optimized quotation and SEM-ready landing page framework.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Multi-Product Cover Showcase:** Detailed sections for Motor, Medical, Pension & Life Schemes, Fire & Domestic Packages, Marine & Cargo, and specialized commercial lines (WIBA & CAR).
- **Interactive UI Components:** Built-in hero banner image slider, mobile-responsive layout, and a floating WhatsApp quick-chat integration for instant client communication.
- **Client Journal / Blog:** Articles and guides explaining insurance terms, claims processes, and requirements in plain language.
- **SEM Lead Generation Form:** Integrated quote request form pre-wired with hidden UTM tracking parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) to support Google and Meta ad campaigns.
- **Local SEO & Trust Signals:** Structured data (Schema.org JSON-LD), GEO meta tags for Nairobi / Thika Road, and clear regulatory compliance indicators.
